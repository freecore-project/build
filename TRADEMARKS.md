FreeCORE carries the TrueNAS® CORE 13.3 system forward as an independently
maintained operating system on FreeBSD. It is not affiliated with or endorsed
by iXsystems, Inc.

## Marks that are not ours

- **TrueNAS®** and **iXsystems®** are trademarks of iXsystems, Inc. They appear
  in this project only nominatively — to state accurately what FreeCORE is
  derived from, and inside preserved upstream copyright and licence texts. They
  are not used in FreeCORE's branding, logos, or user-facing chrome.
- **FreeBSD®** is a registered trademark of the FreeBSD Foundation. FreeCORE
  runs on FreeBSD and says so; it does not use the FreeBSD logo or the Beastie
  mascot as a product mark.

## What we changed, and what we deliberately did not

De-branding a fork means removing another party's trademarks from user-facing
chrome. It does **not** mean stripping copyright notices or licence texts —
those are preserved everywhere, unchanged.

Some upstream-derived names are kept on purpose, as attribution rather than
oversight: internal branch identifiers that carry the project's lineage, the
About dialog's attribution text, and links to upstream documentation. These
are intentional and should not be "cleaned up".

Update trains are not in that group. They are named for the product —
the public product train is `FreeCORE-15.0-STABLE` — because a user picks it
by name in the web interface.

## Our own mark

The FreeCORE name and logo are the project's. If you redistribute a modified
build, please do not present it as FreeCORE.
